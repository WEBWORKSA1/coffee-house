How to use these brewing guides

Every guide on this page follows the same structure so you can compare methods quickly: a key-facts box with the starting ratio, dose, water temperature, grind and total time; a numbered method; a dialing-in section that explains which single variable to change first; and a troubleshooting table that maps what you taste to what you should adjust.

If you are new to brewing, start with the French press or AeroPress — both are forgiving and teach you how grind size and steep time change flavor. When you want more clarity and nuance, move to a pour-over cone or Chemex. Espresso rewards the most practice and the best grinder; read the grinder buying guide before you invest.

Whatever you brew, three habits make the biggest difference: weigh your coffee and water with a scale, grind fresh right before brewing, and use clean, filtered water (see water for coffee). Our brew ratio calculator and brew timer do the math and the counting for you.

Frequently asked questions

Is Coffee.House free to use?

Yes. Every guide, recipe and tool is free and supported by advertising, reader donations and partners. There are no paywalls.

Who writes Coffee.House?

An independent editorial team of coffee enthusiasts and café-industry contributors. Read our editorial policy to see how guides are researched, written and updated.

Can you help me open a coffee shop?

Yes. Request a free launch consultation and we'll reply with a tailored starting plan and, if you want them, introductions to equipment, financing and wholesale coffee partners.

No. Coffee.House is an independent publication and is not affiliated with any café chain, roaster or brand. See our trademark and copyright disclosure.

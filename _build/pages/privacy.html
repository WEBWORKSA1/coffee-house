Privacy policy

Last updated {{UPDATED}}. This policy explains what information Coffee.House (“we”, “us”) collects when you visit coffee.house, how we use it and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you submit a form — newsletter, launch consultation, contest entry, directory listing, pledge, application or contact — we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email address, phone number (if provided), location and message.
  • Automatically collected information. Like most websites, our hosting provider and third-party services may receive technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited and referring URLs.
  • Information stored in your browser. We use your browser's local storage to remember your theme, cookie choice and saved items. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

2. How we use information

We use the information to respond to your requests, send newsletters you asked for, run contests, publish verified directory listings, introduce you to partners only when you opt in, improve the site, prevent abuse and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Advertising (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Choosing “Essential only” in our cookie notice requests non-personalized ads. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland may also see Google's consent message.

4. Third-party services

  • Form processing: form submissions are delivered to us by a third-party form relay service, which processes the data you submit to transmit it.
  • YouTube: videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode and load only after you click play; YouTube's privacy policy then applies.
  • Google Fonts: fonts are loaded from Google's servers, which receive your IP address.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical request data.
  • Payments: if you donate, payments are handled by the payment provider shown; we never see or store card details.

5. Retention and security

We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose you sent them, or as required by law, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect them. No method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or PIPEDA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your personal information, to object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. To make a request, use our contact form. You can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in each email.

7. Children

Coffee.House is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. Contests are open only to adults.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will revise the date above when we do. Questions? Reach us through the contact page. See also our cookie policy.

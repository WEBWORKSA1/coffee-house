Our mission

Coffee is one of the world's most loved drinks, yet good information about brewing it — and about building a business around it — is scattered, sponsored or locked behind courses. We publish clear, precise and free guidance: the exact numbers, the reasons behind them and the fixes when something goes wrong.

What you'll find

  • Brew guides for eight methods, with troubleshooting tables.
  • Recipes for café classics, hot and iced.
  • Beans & origins, from processing to storage.
  • Gear guides that explain trade-offs, not paid rankings.
  • Café business playbooks and a free launch consultation.
  • Free tools, a video library and monthly contests.

How we're funded

Coffee.House is supported by Google AdSense advertising, reader donations, clearly labeled sponsorships and opt-in partner introductions for café founders. None of these buy editorial outcomes — see our editorial policy.

Editorial policy

Our promise: practical, accurate coffee guidance written for readers — never for advertisers.

How we research

Guides are built from established brewing practice, industry standards bodies, academic and agricultural research organizations, and the hands-on experience of baristas, roasters and café operators. Numbers — ratios, temperatures, timings — are given as starting points with the reasoning behind them, so you can adapt them.

How we cover gear

We explain categories, features and trade-offs rather than publishing paid rankings. We do not claim hands-on testing unless we've done it, and we never publish invented reviews, ratings or testimonials.

Health and business topics

Health content reflects broad scientific consensus, avoids claims about curing or treating anything, and includes a not-medical-advice notice. Business cost figures are presented as ranges with assumptions, and we remind readers to verify local rules with professionals.

Independence

Ads are served automatically by Google AdSense and are separate from editorial decisions. Sponsored content is always labeled. Partners never buy verdicts or editorial approval, and founders are introduced to partners only when they opt in.

Updates and corrections

Every guide shows when it was last updated. Found an error? Tell us on the contact page and we'll review and correct it promptly.

Use of tools

Our team may use software tools, including AI-assisted drafting and editing, as part of the writing process. Every published page is reviewed against this policy for accuracy, originality and usefulness.

    How this estimate works

    Each drink is assigned a typical caffeine value — for example, about 63 mg for a single espresso and about 95 mg for a 240 ml cup of drip coffee. The calculator then applies exponential decay: with a 5-hour half-life, 100 mg consumed at 18:00 leaves about 50 mg at 23:00. Slide the half-life to see how sensitive the bedtime number is to your own metabolism.

    Real-world caffeine varies a lot. Robusta beans carry roughly double the caffeine of arabica, larger servings scale linearly, and cold brew concentrate can be very strong before dilution. Our caffeine guide breaks down the ranges by drink and brew method.

    Caffeine and sleep

    Because caffeine blocks adenosine — the molecule that builds "sleep pressure" through the day — a late afternoon coffee can still be active at midnight. Many people find a personal cut-off time 8–10 hours before bed works well. If you love the ritual in the evening, modern decaf keeps the flavor with only a few milligrams of caffeine per cup.

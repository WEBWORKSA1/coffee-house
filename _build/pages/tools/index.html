Why we build tools

Good coffee is mostly arithmetic and repetition: the right ratio, the right timing, then doing it the same way tomorrow. Calculators remove the mental math so you can focus on tasting. Every tool links to the guide that explains the reasoning behind it, so you learn the principle rather than just copying a number.

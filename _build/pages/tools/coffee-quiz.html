How the quiz matches you

Each answer adds weight to the methods that suit it best — for example, preferring bright, fruity flavors favors pour-over, while wanting a week's supply in the fridge favors cold brew. The method with the most weight wins, and we pair it with an origin and recipe that showcase it. There's no wrong answer; many people own two or three brewers for different moods.

The shortest path to opening day

Start with the 12-step roadmap to see the whole journey. Then build your numbers: estimate costs with the startup costs guide, price your menu with the pricing guide, and test the result in the profit calculator. Only when the numbers work at conservative traffic should you shortlist locations using the site-selection scorecard.

From there the work becomes operational: equipment, sourcing, hiring and marketing. Each has its own guide. Remember that permits, health codes, employment law and taxes vary by country and city — confirm requirements with your local authorities and qualified professionals.

Getting café results at home

Most café drinks are built from two components: a concentrated coffee base and milk prepared a specific way. A latte, cappuccino, flat white and cortado can all start from the same double shot — what changes is the volume of milk and how much air you whip into it. Learn the milk once and the whole menu opens up.

No espresso machine? A moka pot or a strong AeroPress concentrate makes a convincing base for most milk drinks, and a French press doubles as a manual milk frother. Every recipe card on this site scales with the + and − buttons, prints cleanly, and lets you tick off ingredients as you go.

For iced drinks, brew stronger than you would for hot coffee or use a cold brew concentrate so melting ice doesn't leave you with a watery cup. The cold brew guide and the cold brew vs iced coffee explainer cover the details.

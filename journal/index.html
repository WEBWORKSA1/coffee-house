About the journal

The journal is where we go beyond the recipe: the chemistry of caffeine and decaffeination, the history and culture of the drink, the economics behind a fair price for farmers, and the sensory skills that help you describe what you taste.

Health topics are written conservatively and reflect broad scientific consensus rather than single studies. They are general information, not medical advice — always talk with a qualified clinician about your own situation.

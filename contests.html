Tips from the judges

For latte art, a clean, well-textured microfoam matters more than a complicated pattern — see latte art basics. For photos, shoot near a window with side light and keep the background simple. For recipes, test your drink three times and write quantities in grams and milliliters so anyone can repeat it.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free to enter?

Yes. No purchase or payment is ever required to enter or win.

Who can enter?

Entrants must be 18 or older (or the age of majority where they live) and must own the rights to their entry. Void where prohibited.

How are winners chosen?

A panel scores eligible entries on the criteria listed for each contest. Winners are notified by email and announced on this page.

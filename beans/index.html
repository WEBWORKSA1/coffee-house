Why origin, process and roast matter

Coffee is the seed of a cherry, and its flavor is decided long before it reaches your grinder. Variety and altitude set the potential; processing — washed, natural, honey or wet-hulled — pushes the cup toward clarity or toward fruit and body; and roasting decides how much of that origin character survives.

Use the origin pages to understand what a label is telling you: typical altitude, harvest season, the varieties you will see, and the flavor tendencies of each country. Then use the roast levels and processing methods guides to predict how a specific bag will taste.

Freshness and preparation finish the job. The grind size chart, storage guide and water guide cover the three things home brewers most often get wrong.

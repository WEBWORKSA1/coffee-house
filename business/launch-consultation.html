Before you open: the essentials

Most cafés that struggle do so because of decisions made before opening day: a lease that's too expensive for the foot traffic, a build-out that eats the working-capital reserve, or a menu priced without knowing the cost of each cup. The 12-step roadmap walks through each decision in order, and the startup costs guide shows typical ranges by format.

Bring three things to any conversation with a landlord, lender or partner: a one-page concept, a realistic monthly profit-and-loss model, and a list of the equipment you need. The business plan guide and equipment checklist give you both.

Frequently asked questions

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. The written starting plan is free with no obligation. If you ask for partner quotes, partners may pay us a referral fee — never you — and we only introduce you with your permission.

What happens after I submit?

We review your answers and reply by email within two business days with a tailored starting plan, the biggest risks we see, and next steps. If you opted in, we arrange introductions to relevant partners.

Do you work outside the United States?

Yes. We help founders worldwide. Regulations, permits, taxes and costs vary by country and city, so we'll flag what to verify locally.

Will you share my information?

Only with partners you explicitly ask to hear from. See our privacy policy for details.

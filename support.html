Why reader support matters

Advertising covers part of our costs, but it can't fund everything a great coffee resource needs: commissioning experienced baristas to review guides, producing video lessons, running fair contests with real prizes and building free tools. Donations let us say yes to that work without paywalls or sponsored rankings.

Coffee.House is an independent publication, not a charity, so contributions are not tax-deductible. Payments are processed by third-party providers; we never see or store card details.

Frequently asked questions

Where does my donation go?

Into four buckets: operations (hosting, tools, editing), new content and videos, promotion and outreach, and community contests and prizes. We publish the split on this page and update it as we grow.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

No. Coffee.House is an independent publication, not a registered charity, so contributions are not tax-deductible.

Can I sponsor a specific contest or guide?

Yes. Use the form and choose 'Contests & prizes' or 'New guides & videos', or see our sponsorship options on the advertise page.

How does pledge mode work?

When instant checkout isn't shown, submit a pledge and we'll email you a secure payment link for the amount and frequency you chose.
